# Knowledge Score Distribution

The histogram below displays the distribution of the newly calculated weighted knowledge scores across all 201 participants. The distribution is heavily right-skewed, meaning that most participants had lower knowledge scores, while a small minority (like the best participant) achieved exceptionally high scores.

![Knowledge Score Distribution](charts/Knowledge_Score_Distribution.png)

### Key Observations
- **Right Skew:** The vast majority of participants scored between 1.0 and 4.0.
- **Median:** Half of the participants scored below 2.89.
- **Outliers:** The best participant (Score: 10.3) and a few others form a long tail of highly knowledgeable outliers on the far right.
