# Sorted Knowledge Scores Dot Plot (Cluster Analysis)

The dot plot below visualizes the `Weighted_Knowledge_Score` for all 201 participants, sorted from lowest to highest. 

By plotting the scores this way, sudden vertical jumps or "plateaus" (flat horizontal sections) can indicate distinct clusters or tiers of knowledge among the participants.

![Knowledge Score Dot Plot](charts/Knowledge_Score_DotPlot.png)

### Interpretation Guide:
- **Smooth Curves:** If the dots form a relatively smooth, continuous curve, it suggests a gradual spectrum of knowledge rather than strictly separated groups.
- **Plateaus (Flat areas):** These indicate large groups of people who achieved very similar or identical scores, forming a tight cluster.
- **Vertical Jumps:** A sudden vertical gap between dots indicates a significant separation between tiers.

**Observation:** Look at the right side of the graph—you can clearly see the curve steepen dramatically, showing that the top ~10-15% of participants form a distinct "high-knowledge" cluster separate from the rest of the cohort.
