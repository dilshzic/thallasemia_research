# Thalassemia Survey - Marriage & Partner Screening Report (Part C: Q30-Q34)

This report presents the attitudes, disclosure practices, and pre-marital screening choices of the **201 participants**.

> [!NOTE]
> In this cohort, **65.17% (131/201) of participants are single**, while **34.83% (70/201) are married**. This demographic split explains the high number of 'Missing/No Response' or 'Other' (e.g. 'Still not married') for partner screening questions.
