# Thalassemia Expanded Knowledge Scores & Z-Scores Report

This report documents the calculation of the **Expanded Knowledge Score** and standardized Z-scores for all **201 participants** in the cohort. Unlike the basic score, this version utilizes **all 11 general knowledge questions** in Part B of the questionnaire.

## 1. Methodology: How the Expanded Knowledge Score is Calculated

The **Expanded Knowledge Score** is constructed as a composite score of correct answers across all 11 knowledge questions. The maximum possible score is **20 points** (12 points from single-select questions and 8 points from multi-select options). The criteria are:

| Question | Question Title / Concept | Correct Answer Criteria | Points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Q15** | Is thalassemia a blood-related disease? | Selects `Yes` | 1 |
| **Q16** | What are the clinical forms of thalassemia? | Sum of checked correct forms (Major, Minor, Intermedia) | 0 to 3 |
| **Q17** | What is the most severe form of thalassemia? | Selects `Thalassemia major (severe form)` | 1 |
| **Q19** | Does thalassemia major require lifelong treatment? | Selects `Yes` | 1 |
| **Q20** | Can thalassemia major be cured? | Selects `Very difficult (e.g., bone marrow transplant)` | 1 |
| **Q21** | Can the spread of thalassemia be prevented? | Selects `Can be prevented` | 1 |
| **Q22** | How is thalassemia transmitted? | Selects `From generation to generation (hereditary)` | 1 |
| **Q23** | Is a thalassemia carrier usually sick or healthy? | Selects `Healthy` | 1 |
| **Q24** | A child born from two thalassemia carriers will be: | Selects `Has a chance to be affected (e.g., 25%)` | 1 |
| **Q26** | Births in Sri Lanka per year | Selects `40–100` | 1 |
| **Q27** | Problems faced by thalassemia major patients | Sum of checked correct complications (8 options) | 0 to 8 |
| **Total** | **Max Theoretical Score** | - | **20** |

## 2. Methodology: Z-Score Calculations

Standardized Z-scores show how many standard deviations a participant's score is from the cohort mean:

$$Z_i = \frac{X_i - \mu}{\sigma}$$

Where:
- $Z_i$ is the Z-score for participant $i$
- $X_i$ is the raw Expanded Knowledge Score for participant $i$
- $\mu$ is the cohort mean expanded score: **`9.3234`**
- $\sigma$ is the cohort sample standard deviation ($N - 1$ degrees of freedom): **`3.9446`**
