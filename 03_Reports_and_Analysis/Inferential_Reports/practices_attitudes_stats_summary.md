### Key Interpretations
1. **Pre-marital Screening Importance (Q32)**: Individuals who believe pre-marital screening is 'Very important' or 'Important' tend to have significantly higher knowledge scores than those who are unsure or believe it is not important.
2. **Carrier Marriage Acceptance (Q31)**: Knowledge scores do not show a statistically significant difference between those who accept, reject, or are unsure about marriages between two carriers.
3. **Partner Screening Practice (Q33)**: Those who screened their partners *before* marriage have higher knowledge scores than those who did not screen or did not disclose carrier status, indicating that proactive screening is associated with higher health literacy.
4. **Relatives Screening Extent (Q37)**: Higher screening penetration among first-degree relatives is strongly associated with higher difficulty-weighted knowledge scores of the respondent.
